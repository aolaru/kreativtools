Best fit

  • Text-based PDFs with selectable text already inside the document
  • Contracts, drafts, handoffs, and exports where readable structure matters more than exact layout
  • Quick DOCX conversion without upload limits or third-party document quotas

Important limit

This converter rebuilds structured text with heading and paragraph heuristics. It does not preserve exact PDF visuals, and scanned image-only PDFs will need OCR elsewhere before they convert well.

After export

Open the DOCX in Word, Pages, or Google Docs to continue editing. If the original PDF is still too large to send, use PDF Compress on the source file first.

Need exact form filling instead?

Use PDF Fill & Sign when the goal is to place text and signatures on top of a fixed PDF layout instead of converting it into an editable document.
