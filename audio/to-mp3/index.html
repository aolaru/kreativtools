Audio to MP3

Convert WAV and other browser-supported audio files into MP3 and choose how aggressively you want to compress the final export.

Best for

Turning heavier WAV files into lighter MP3 downloads for sharing, previews, drafts, and smaller everyday delivery files.

Compression rule

Lower bitrate means smaller MP3 files and more compression. Higher bitrate means better quality and larger downloads.
