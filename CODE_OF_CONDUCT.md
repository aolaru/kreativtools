# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in this project is expected to communicate respectfully and focus on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive when reporting problems or reviewing changes.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Respect privacy. Do not share personal information, private files, credentials, or sensitive documents.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, sharing private information without permission, or repeated bad-faith conduct is not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report a conduct concern privately to [hello@madebykreativ.com](mailto:hello@madebykreativ.com). Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or restrict participation when this policy is violated.
