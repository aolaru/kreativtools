A desktop app idea

File work, without the upload.

We're exploring a simple desktop app for preparing PDFs and images on your own computer. No account, no subscription, and no internet needed for file processing are the goals.

Join the waitlist by email Explore the idea

This is an idea, not an available app. No payment is required to join.

Proposed one-time price €24

One payment, not a subscription.

A price we're testing, not a final offer. Taxes, included updates, and supported platforms are not yet decided.

The idea

A simpler way to prepare files.

For freelancers and small teams who handle the same documents and images again and again.

Everyday PDF tasks

Merge, split, organize, compress, and export pages as images from a single desktop workspace.

Images ready to send

Resize, convert, and compress images without uploading your files to a service.

Less repeated work

We're considering batch processing, saved presets, and repeatable delivery workflows for common jobs.

Designed for local work

The goal is to process files on your device, without accounts or app analytics. We will verify that before making it a product promise.

Audio, video, font, and data tools could be considered later. All app features are proposed; scope, availability, and desktop operating systems depend on what we learn from this waitlist.

A practical example

From a folder of files to a handoff.

Imagine merging a few PDFs, preparing images, and applying a saved export preset before sending everything to a client. The goal is fewer repeated steps, not another online account.
